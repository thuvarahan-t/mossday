# Animated emoji

The animated WebP files in this folder, and the diary mood stills in `../emoji/`
(`disappointed-face.png`, `neutral-face.png`, `slightly-smiling-face.png`,
`grinning-face-with-smiling-eyes.png`, `star-struck.png`), are from
**Noto Emoji Animation** by Google, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source: https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/

Changes: resized to 144 px (animations) and 128 px (single-frame stills) and re-encoded.
